ARJUNA — physical archive tracking for a land registry office

In production · a district land office in West Java
Laravel 12MySQLBlade + AlpineWindows Service installerOffline-first LAN

Problem

501,335 land title numbers across 26 sub-districts and 352 villages, with their title books and survey documents on shelves. The national system records the data but not where the physical sheet is or who is holding it. Staff spent minutes per lookup and lost track of loans.

What I built

  • Binder → slot → side location model that answers "where is title #X" for every number in a binder's range before a single document is entered
  • Loan, return, overdue and stock-take workflows with printed receipts and QR binder labels
  • Column-mapping importer for inconsistent exports from different offices
  • 19 reports, daily backups, one-click updater, Windows Service installer that survives reboots

Result

5,896 binders imported and labeled. Lookup under a minute. Installed on an office PC with no internet, used from phones over office Wi-Fi. Source handed over with a signed license.

  • 470,989 titles (94%) checked against the shelves; 46,262 recorded as physically missing, a figure the office never had before
  • Zero personal data stored: the importer never reads the 458,940 rows of holder names in the source exports
  • 92% of historical loans linked to their documents automatically; 3,007 flagged for a person to review instead of guessed
  • Village is taken from the code inside the title number, not the typed name: one village spelled two ways in the source (19,371 vs 4,425 records) still lands in one place

v1.1.0 · licensed, field sales
GoStandard library onlyVanilla JSEd25519 licensingSingle .exe

Problem

Owners of Indonesian boarding houses (kost) track rooms, tenants and monthly rent in notebooks and WhatsApp chats. Every tenant has a different move-in date, so due dates are scattered and late payments slip through.

What I built

  • Room map by floor, check-in with printed rental agreement, check-out with deposit settlement
  • Rent invoices issued automatically from each tenant's move-in date (monthly to yearly), with Wi-Fi, parking and late fees
  • Due-date reminder page: filter "due in exactly N days" or "within N days", then send one by one from the owner's own WhatsApp; the app records who was reminded
  • Add-on modules (cash book, electricity & water meters, bookings, maintenance tickets) unlocked by signed license codes

Result

Owner and caretaker work at the same time from a PC and phones on the kost Wi-Fi, without internet. Shipped with pricing by room count, brochure, narrated demo video and a sales commission scheme.
